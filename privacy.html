Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Timetoshoot makes short memory films from photos and videos you send us. This page explains exactly what we collect when you book, why we collect it, where it is kept, and how to have it removed. It is written in plain language on purpose.

The short version. We collect your name and WhatsApp number so we can talk to you about your film, plus the details of the booking you made. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with anyone who is not helping us make or deliver your film.

What we collect when you book

The booking form on this site asks for, and records:

WhatWhy
Your nameSo we know who we are speaking to
Your WhatsApp numberTo confirm your booking and send your film
Occasion and, if you give one, the date of that occasionTo shape the film and make sure it is ready in time
Film length and the date and time slot you chooseTo hold your slot and price the work
Whether you follow us on InstagramOnly to apply the follower discount
Language you used the site inSo we reply in the same language

You tick a box confirming you agree to be contacted on WhatsApp before the form will accept your booking. That consent is recorded with the booking.

Information about how you arrived

If you reached this site from an advertisement, the link usually carries campaign information. We record it with your booking so we know which advertisement brought you here. This is campaign information, not personal information: typically a campaign name, an advertisement id and a click identifier supplied by the advertising platform.

Anonymous usage counts

We count how far visitors get through the booking form, so we can see where people give up and fix it. These counts record a random session code that changes every visit, which step was reached, and the language. They carry no name, no number and nothing that identifies you.

Advertising measurement

When advertising is running, this site may load the Meta Pixel. It tells Meta that a visit or a booking happened, so the advertising can be measured and improved. Meta handles that information under its own privacy policy. The Pixel does not receive your name or your phone number from us.

The photos and videos you send

Photos and videos are not uploaded on this website. You send them to us on WhatsApp after we confirm your booking, so they travel through WhatsApp, under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

We use what you send only to make your film. We will never post your photos, your film, or your family publicly without asking you first and getting a clear yes.

Where your information is kept

Bookings are stored in a private Google Sheet belonging to Timetoshoot, hosted by Google. Access is limited to the people at Timetoshoot who need it to make and deliver your film. The website itself is hosted by Vercel. We do not store your information anywhere else, and this site has no account for you to create and no password of yours to lose.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records while we are working on your film and afterwards as a record of the work, so we can help you if you come back. Bookings that are never confirmed expire on their own and are marked as such.

You can ask us to delete your details at any time and we will remove them, unless we are required to keep a record of a payment.

What we never do

  • We do not sell your information to anyone.
  • We do not pass your number to other businesses or to marketing lists.
  • We do not send you unrelated promotional messages because you booked once.
  • We do not ask for payment details on this website.

Your choices

You can ask us, on WhatsApp, to:

  • tell you what we hold about you
  • correct anything that is wrong
  • delete your details
  • stop contacting you

We will do it, and we will not make it difficult.

Children

This service is meant for adults booking films, often about their families. We do not knowingly take bookings from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is WhatsApp. Tell us what you would like us to do with your information and we will confirm once it is done.
